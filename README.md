**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **STAR WARS Zero Company™**. A single-player trainer for STAR WARS Zero Company that modifies operator health, Action Points, Advantage, squad resources, and mission progression systems during tactical operations and base activities.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to explore the campaign, experiment with operator abilities and squad compositions, reduce mission difficulty, or replay tactical operations with fewer resource and injury constraints.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Each operator receives three Action Points per turn. Movement consumes AP according to distance and terrain, and available destinations are shown on the battlefield. Use the trainer's Action Point option to reposition freely, reach cover, set up flanking attacks, or combine movement with additional attacks and abilities.

### Tools & Inventory

Operators can be customized with different appearances, loadouts, and abilities. Use unlimited credits when adjusting supported squad equipment or other campaign purchases. Keep the trainer disabled while changing equipment if values fail to refresh immediately, then enable it after returning to the tactical or base screen.

### World & Environment

The Den is Zero Company's base of operations, where Hawks selects missions and operations from the Holotable, manages the squad, and develops relationships between teammates. Missions advance the current cycle, while non-combat operations consume Intel and can provide items, credits, influence, and bond effects. Unlimited Intel and Credits are most useful before confirming these activities.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat is turn-based, and every operator uses AP to move, attack, call for backup, enter Overwatch, rally a downed ally, or activate special abilities. Operators gain Advantage by damaging enemies. Health, Action Points, Advantage, injury protection, and enemy-strength options should be activated after loading into a mission and disabled before returning to menus or saving whenever possible.
